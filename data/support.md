# 支持与退款

## 标准支持

所有付费用户可以通过 support@northstar.example 获得邮件支持，首次回复时间不超过 48 小时。

## 企业支持

企业版包含工单系统和企业微信群。严重故障的首次回复时间不超过 4 小时。月度可用性目标为 99.9%。

## 退款

自付费开通之日起 7 日内可以申请全额退款。超过 7 日的，按未使用的整月比例退款。已经开具增值税专用发票的，需要先退回发票再办理退款。

## English

Paid users can email support@northstar.example. Standard support sends a first response within 48 hours. Enterprise support includes a ticket system and a WeCom group, with a first response within 4 hours for severe incidents and a monthly availability target of 99.9%. A full refund is available within 7 days of paid activation. After 7 days, refunds cover unused whole months. If a special VAT invoice was issued, it must be returned before the refund is processed.
