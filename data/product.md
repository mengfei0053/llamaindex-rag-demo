# 岚图协作（Lantu Collab）

岚图协作是北辰软件的团队协作产品，包含在线文档、任务看板和团队知识库。

## 版本与价格

- 免费版：最多 5 名成员，1 GB 存储空间。
- 专业版：最多 50 名成员，每人每月 99 元，100 GB 存储空间。
- 企业版：成员数量不限，价格需联系销售，包含专属成功经理。

## 数据驻留

客户可以选择将数据存放在中国大陆（上海）或新加坡。创建团队时选定区域，之后如需迁移需要提交工单。

## English

Lantu Collab is Northstar Software's teamwork product. It includes docs, task boards, and a team knowledge base. The Free plan allows up to 5 members and 1 GB of storage. The Professional plan allows up to 50 members, costs CNY 99 per user per month, and includes 100 GB of storage. The Enterprise plan has unlimited members and is priced by sales; it includes a dedicated success manager. Data can reside in Shanghai (mainland China) or Singapore. The region is chosen when the team is created, and a later move requires a support ticket.
